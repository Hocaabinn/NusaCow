## 🌟 Vision
**NusaCow** is a decentralized Real-World Asset (RWA) protocol designed to bridge the gap between traditional Indonesian livestock farming and global decentralized finance (DeFi). We empower local ranchers with accessible funding while providing investors with transparent, asset-backed, and high-yield investment opportunities.

By leveraging Solana's high-performance infrastructure, we transform physical cattle into liquid, fractionalized digital assets.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. Fractional Livestock Ownership
No more barriers to entry. Through tokenization, a single head of cattle can be owned by multiple investors, allowing for low-ticket entry into high-yield agricultural cycles.

### 2. On-Chain Identity (cNFTs)
Every cow in the NusaCow ecosystem is minted as a **Compressed NFT (cNFT)** on Solana. This ensures a cost-effective way to store immutable metadata, including breed, health records, and geolocation.

### 3. Transparent Proof of Growth
We implement a "Field-to-Blockchain" transparency layer. Partner ranchers provide periodic updates—weight logs and health photos—that are hashed and recorded on-chain, ensuring investors can track their asset's growth in real-time.

### 4. Automated Settlement Vaults
Using **Program Derived Addresses (PDAs)**, we ensure that all funds are handled securely and non-custodially. Profits are distributed automatically via smart contracts once the livestock reaches its maturity and is sold.

## 🛠 Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** Next.js 14, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
- **Animations:** Framer Motion (Premium "Modern Pastoral" Aesthetic)
- **Blockchain:** Solana (Anchor Framework, SPL-Token, Metaplex Bubblegum for cNFTs)
- **State Management:** TanStack Query, Recoil
- **Backend/Storage:** Supabase, IPFS (for metadata & images)

## 🛤 Protocol Journey
1. **Asset Onboarding:** Verified ranchers list their cattle.
2. **cNFT Minting:** Digital twin created on Solana.
3. **Vault Funding:** Investors supply USDC liquidity.
4. **Active Growth:** Real-world fattening phase with on-chain tracking.
5. **Final Settlement:** Asset sale and automated profit distribution.
